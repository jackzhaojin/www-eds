How each score was reached

  1. Brand expression 78

    Lavender family is 45% of painted pixels on the home page and Playfair carries a distinct display register (90 anchor traits), but six radii are in use and the explainers run their own token layer (70 anchor tension). Evidence F-010, F-013.

  2. Visual hierarchy and craft 64

    Ad-hoc type ladder (40 anchor marker) offset by a single clear focal point and a what/who/ask hero in one viewport; secondary pages vary title caps and the writing index flattens hierarchy. Evidence F-007, F-008, F-009, F-011.

  3. Conversion focus 74

    One destination-named primary CTA in the first viewport and real proof (Adobe announcement, issuer verification links), no bucket fragmentation; the home underweights the writing and hubs end inconsistently. Evidence F-003, F-006.

  4. Accessibility 84

    14 of 15 palette pairs pass AA in role (one at 4.49); 99% of content images carry meaningful alt; full landmarks and exactly one h1 on 34/34 pages; reduced motion honored; duplicate links per credential and small article targets remain. Evidence F-015, F-020.

  5. Technical SEO 86

    Robots, sitemap (34 with lastmod), self canonicals, single-hop https, unique titles, descriptions, complete OG and page-type JSON-LD on every page; 19 titles exceed 60 characters and email obfuscation plants a 404 link. Evidence F-016, F-017.

  6. Content and LLM visibility 88

    llms.txt present, served = rendered words (99-100% strict on 7 pages), Person + TechArticle + VideoObject + credential schema, article median 2387 extractable words with specific claims; only 11% of headings read as questions. Evidence F-025.

  7. Performance (Core Web Vitals) 50

    Mobile TBT (lab INP proxy) is poor on / (1182 ms) and /portfolio/ (3962 ms), which sets the 40 anchor; LCP and CLS are good on every page and desktop is good everywhere, so the score sits above the anchor. Evidence F-001, F-002, F-004.

2Executive summary

The site's biggest lever is performance, which scores 50, the lowest of the seven dimensions, because two canvases block the main thread on phones: lab TBT on a throttled mobile profile is 1182 ms on the home page (F-001) and 3962 ms on the portfolio (F-002), while LCP and CLS are good on every page tested and desktop is good everywhere.

The third P1 is editorial: the home page gives Technical Writing one paragraph and one link, although its articles carry the site's depth, with a median of 2387 extractable words (F-003).

The machine-facing layer is strong: robots, a 34-URL sitemap, self canonicals, JSON-LD on all 34 pages and an llms.txt put technical SEO at 86 and content and LLM visibility at 88, with long titles and Cloudflare email obfuscation as the gaps.

Most remaining design findings are consistency drift (title caps, radii, breakpoints, a private explainer token layer), and 13 of them are register candidates for the replica rather than fixes.

Assumptions

The audit ran without asking questions; these are the calls it made instead, verbatim from audit.json.

  1. Primary conversion assumed to be engagement with the work (portfolio, writing, talks), not a contact form: the site has no form, no booking link and no price.
  2. The audit covers the 34 sitemap pages; the 7 GitHub Pages project sites on the same host are out of scope.
  3. Design findings marked registerCandidate are candidates for the replica's inconsistency register, not fixes; the replica keeps the live design unless a register entry says otherwise.
  4. Lab metrics only: PageSpeed Insights returned 429 (daily quota, no key), so TBT stands in for INP.
  5. Captures are light mode; the dark theme was not scored (impeccable reported 0 AA failures in both themes).

F-001P1Performance (Core Web Vitals)

Home hero canvas blocks the main thread on mobile

Observed
mobile TBT 1182 ms (poor) on /; desktop 27 ms. Critique trace: page-art.js resize() builds a blur(14px) copy mask in a 620-740 ms task at 4x CPU, also under reduced motion.measurement: measurements.cwv
Impact
The flagship demo targets Lighthouse 100; a poor mobile TBT is the one metric that misses it on the home page.
Fix
Build the mask in idle time after LCP and skip it under reduced motion; cap the per-frame cost.
F-002P1Performance (Core Web Vitals)

Portfolio signal-field canvas janks phones

Observed
mobile TBT 3962 ms (poor), 77 long tasks on /portfolio/; desktop 0 ms. Critique: #signal-field loop in portfolio/script.js.measurement: measurements.cwv
Impact
The portfolio is the site's proof page; a phone visitor gets a stuttering first scroll.
Fix
Throttle the signal field to visible frames and pause it off-screen; start after first interaction on mobile.
